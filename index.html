Privacy Policy — Rugby Performance Analytics

Effective 23 November 2026

Rugby Performance Analytics is a personal, non-commercial application intended to analyse the operator's rugby training, fitness, sleep and recovery. The application is under development; automated collection is not yet active.

Information collected

After explicit WHOOP authorisation, the application may retrieve profile information, sleep, recovery, physiological cycles, strain and workout records. Separately authorised Garmin records and manually logged training results may also be processed.

Purpose

Information is used for personal training history, rugby performance analysis, recovery trends and private reports. It is not intended for medical diagnosis.

Storage and processing

Once enabled, records are intended to be held in a private access-controlled Supabase database. Infrastructure providers may process data solely to provide hosting and storage. The public privacy policy website contains no personal fitness records.

Sharing

Personal health and fitness information is not sold or used for advertising. It is not intentionally shared with marketers.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Data is retained for longitudinal analysis until deleted or the application is retired. WHOOP authorisation can be revoked through WHOOP's authorised application settings. Revocation prevents further authorised access but does not automatically erase records already stored. For deletion of stored records or questions, contact the operator at flynnmcdermott9@outlook.com.

Security

The application is designed to use OAuth and access-restricted storage. No online system can guarantee complete security. Data collection will not begin until authentication and storage setup is completed.

Changes

This policy may change as the application evolves. Updates will be published at this URL.
